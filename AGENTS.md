# Rules for AI collaborators — KITT LED Strip Car Mod

Dale owns the project. This file sets the collaboration rules and the project's place in the org,
in the same convention as Dale's other repos.

## Place in the org
- **Dale** — owner.
- **Claudius Maximus** — general overseer of all of Dale's interconnected projects; works for Dale
  only. It may act on behalf of any agent here (owner, manager, engineers, helpers): read, branch,
  push, comment, and open or merge PRs within this file's rules. What is reserved for Dale stays Dale's.
- This is a **standalone project** (not under the Redux Manager).
- **Custom Hardware Design** — a shared support project, available for general support.
- Roles to be defined.

## Hard rules
- **Dale decides** anything irreversible or anything that touches the vehicle: wiring into the
  car's electrical system, fusing, permanent fixings, anything drawing from the battery or ignition.
  Ask as a plain yes/no question and show the exact change first.
- **Read-only first.** Inspect and propose, then change only with approval.
- **No secrets, ever.** No tokens, keys, passwords or account numbers.
- **Notes are facts.** Record what Dale said or what was measured; mark guesses as such.
- **Safety:** LED strips and 12 V automotive wiring must be fused and reversible; never wire into
  anything safety-related (brakes, airbags, steering, lighting the law requires) without Dale's
  explicit decision.
- **Brevity:** result first, at most 5 lines unless the work needs more.

## Dale decides
Anything irreversible, anything on the vehicle, and the rule files (`AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`).
